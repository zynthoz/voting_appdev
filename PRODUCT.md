# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Members of cooperatives or classes (voters) casting their votes in local elections; organizers setting up candidate profiles and managing election parameters; and system administrators auditing activity logs and managing users.

## Product Purpose
To provide a clear, reliable, and accessible web-based voting platform that enables organizations to conduct elections smoothly, ensuring voters can cast their ballots with confidence and administrators can manage records efficiently.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, civic, transparent. The voice is objective, professional, and reassuringly simple, avoiding marketing hype or gamification.

## Anti-references
- Gamified consumer apps (no animations, no celebratory confetti, no hype).
- Cluttered SaaS dashboards (no complex hero metrics, no neon charts, no dark mode crypto/cyberpunk aesthetics).
- Decorative side-stripe borders, floating card grids, or unnecessary modal windows.

## Design Principles
- **Trust through Clarity**: Visual elements must emphasize legibility and structure. High contrast and clean spacing reassure users of the system's reliability.
- **Task-First Flow**: Minimize cognitive load. The UI should guide users directly to their immediate task (e.g., selecting a candidate, viewing results) without distraction.
- **Calm, Accessible Utility**: Use a restrained color palette (tinted neutrals with a single accent) and clean typographic scale to ensure maximum accessibility across diverse user groups.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance (especially color contrast and text scaling).
- Clear focus indicators and logical tab order for keyboard-only navigation.
- Responsive design adapting gracefully to all viewport sizes down to 320px.
